Thoughtful guidance for your move, shaped around your circumstances.

Who can you support with a move to Dubai?

We support individuals, couples, families and businesses. Whether you are moving on your own, bringing children, setting up a company or simply looking for a more supported landing, we shape the introductions and coordination around your priorities.

Can you help me find an off-plan or ready-to-move-in home?

Yes. We can guide your search for a property that fits your preferred area, style and requirements, whether it is off-plan or ready to move into. Explore our Dubai real estate service for more about buying and renting.

Can you help with company set-up and visa arrangements?

We can help you understand the steps involved and introduce you to experienced independent specialists suited to your situation. They provide the relevant company, immigration or legal advice and handle formal applications. Eligibility, approvals, processing times and provider fees depend on your circumstances and the relevant authorities.

Can you help families with schools and household staff?

Yes. We can make tailored introductions to independent education and staffing specialists, helping you explore school options that suit your preferred curriculum and location, as well as household roles such as nannies, housekeepers, private chefs, chauffeurs and estate managers. Admissions, availability and hiring decisions are made by the relevant providers.

What might relocation support include?

Depending on your needs, support may include a tailored community brochure and an explanation of key points about the area when you purchase a property through us, plus introductions for company set-up, visa coordination, schools, private staffing and other settling-in requirements. We agree the scope around your move rather than assuming every client needs the same services.

How are fees handled?

For clients who purchase a property through Energy Johnson, our relocation guidance is included at no additional Energy Johnson fee. If you are not purchasing through us, we can discuss a bespoke relocation package and agree any Energy Johnson fee with you in advance. Independent providers set and charge for their own services directly. Where a specific arrangement requires a separate Energy Johnson fee, we will explain and agree it before proceeding.

Do you provide legal, visa or school admission decisions?

No. We help you navigate the process and connect you with experienced independent specialists. Formal advice, applications, admissions and approvals are handled by the relevant qualified professional, school or authority. We can stay involved as your point of contact while those steps progress.

Can you support me after I arrive?

Yes. Our role can continue as you settle in, with a personal point of contact and relevant introductions based on what you still need. The scope is tailored to your situation and agreed with you.
