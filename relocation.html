Can you help me find an off-plan property?

Yes. We can guide you through an off-plan search around your preferred location, style, needs and budget. Through our real estate network, we can coordinate introductions and viewings, and connect you with relevant specialists to discuss project details and availability.

Can you help me find a ready-to-move-in home?

Yes. We can help explore homes that are ready to move into, tailored to your preferred area and priorities, then coordinate introductions and viewings through our network.

Can I choose the location where I want to search?

Yes. Your preferred location is the starting point. Tell us where you would like to look, and we can confirm what support our real estate network can provide there.

Is relocation guidance included in the property purchase?

For clients who buy a property through Energy Johnson, our personal relocation guidance is included at no additional Energy Johnson fee. This can include a tailored community brochure, an explanation of key points about the area and introductions to experienced independent specialists for school options and admissions, private staffing, company set-up and other settling-in needs. School places and third-party services are subject to their own criteria, availability and fees; clients pay those providers directly. If you have bought elsewhere, we can discuss a tailored relocation package.

What if I need relocation support but am not buying through Energy Johnson?

We can discuss a bespoke relocation package shaped around your move, family and priorities. Any Energy Johnson fee for that package is explained and agreed before work begins.
