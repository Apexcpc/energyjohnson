RAS AL KHAIMAH

Know the place
before you choose.

Ras Al Khaimah offers a different UAE escape, with a long coastline, resort destinations, mountain landscapes and a growing hospitality scene. Al Marjan Island is one of the emirate’s best-known coastal destinations, with operating resorts as well as further development underway.

Energy Johnson is beginning to grow its concierge offering in Ras Al Khaimah. We can discuss hotel stays, restaurant reservations, private transfers and chauffeur arrangements, yacht or boat experiences, and tailored itineraries for couples, families or small groups. Every request is confirmed against real availability, supplier terms and the access policies of each venue.

Wynn Al Marjan Island is an integrated resort currently planned to open in 2027, according to the resort and local development authority. Its opening date, facilities, booking terms and access policies remain subject to the operator’s updates. We can help plan a wider Ras Al Khaimah stay, but we do not promise access to future facilities or arrange gaming access.
