HOW BOOKINGS WORK

Clear arrangements,
agreed in advance.

For most holidays and private jet or yacht bookings, Energy Johnson does not charge a separate booking fee. Where partner commission applies, it is paid by the provider. Clients pay the selected hotel, restaurant, venue, yacht or aviation provider directly and confirm supplier terms with them.

A specific event or bespoke service may require an organisation fee payable to Energy Johnson. If so, we explain the scope and fee and agree it with you before work begins. Availability, final pricing, deposits, cancellations and supplier terms are confirmed by each provider.

COMMON QUESTIONS

A few useful
answers.

Every brief is individual. Use these points to frame your next conversation.

Can you arrange a holiday if I have not chosen a destination?

Yes. Tell us who is travelling, when, the experience you want and practical preferences. We can discuss destinations and hotels around your brief.

Do you only work with hotels shown on the website?

No. Named hotels are examples, not a fixed selection. We can explore other hotels and destinations worldwide according to your preferences and availability.

Can you coordinate a private jet and yacht for one trip?

We specialise in private jet and yacht bookings and can discuss a package that brings parts of a journey together. Routes, schedules, availability and terms are confirmed for each enquiry.

Who do I pay for my trip?

For most holiday, jet and yacht arrangements, clients pay the provider directly. Energy Johnson may receive a provider commission. Any separate Energy Johnson fee for an event or bespoke service is discussed and agreed in advance.
