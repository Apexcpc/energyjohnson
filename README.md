# Energy Johnson Concierge , website concept

An independent, static multi-page website concept for Energy Johnson. It presents wellness as the central thread across private fitness retreats, relocation support, personal and business consultancy, and lifestyle concierge.

## Pages

- `index.html` , brand introduction, services and preferred retreat destinations
- `about.html` , expertise, vision and approach
- `retreats.html` , fitness retreat experience and destinations
- `relocation.html` , company and personal relocation guidance
- `consultancy.html` , personal and business consultancy
- `concierge.html` , private lifestyle concierge
- `contact.html` , enquiry details and contact options
- `terms.html` , placeholder terms page

Open `index.html` in a browser to preview. Images and fonts load from Unsplash and Google Fonts. Replace the founder image with the supplied portrait and confirm image rights before publishing. The enquiry form currently opens the visitor's email app; connect a form service before launch.

The September 23 to 27, 2026 Marbella date was omitted because it has passed. New retreat dates can be added once confirmed.
