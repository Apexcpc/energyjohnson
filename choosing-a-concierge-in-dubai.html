A PRACTICAL GUIDE · DUBAI

Choosing a concierge
in Dubai.

What to consider when you are looking for private support with travel, experiences or a move.

A THOUGHTFUL PLACE TO START

Choose for your brief,
not a title.

There is no single concierge that is right for every client. The best fit depends on what you need, where you need it and how much support you would like along the way.

Before making an enquiry, consider the service you need, your destination and timing, the level of coordination you expect, and how you prefer to communicate. A clear brief makes it easier to understand whether a provider is suited to your plans.

Use the questions below to compare the scope, process and terms of any private arrangement.

WHAT TO LOOK FOR

Clarity is a mark
of good service.

01 · SCOPE

A relevant speciality

Ask which services the concierge personally coordinates and which are arranged through independent specialists. Make sure the service matches your actual brief, whether that is private travel, relocation or lifestyle support.

02 · PROCESS

Clear communication

Understand who will be your point of contact, how availability is confirmed and when a booking becomes final. Ask how changes, special requests and third-party arrangements are handled.

03 · COSTS

Terms before confirmation

Ask for the proposed scope and costs before proceeding. Clarify what is included, which costs are paid to third parties, and the relevant cancellation or change terms.

The right arrangement depends on your needs, timing and provider availability. Confirm all services, costs and terms directly before booking.

QUESTIONS WORTH ASKING

Before you
make an enquiry.

A few practical questions can help set clear expectations.

Who is the best concierge in Dubai?

There is no universal answer. The right provider is the one whose experience, service scope, location coverage, communication and terms suit your particular plans. Compare the details of the service rather than relying on a broad “best” claim.

How much does a private concierge cost in Dubai?

Pricing depends on the type and scope of the request. For private jet and yacht arrangements, the itinerary, timing, party size and availability can affect the proposal. Ask for the full costs and inclusions before confirming. Energy Johnson does not list fixed package prices publicly and discusses costs privately for each enquiry.

What should I ask before booking a private jet or yacht?

Share your route or destination, preferred dates, party size and priorities. Ask which options are available, what the proposal includes, and what terms apply before confirming a booking.

Can a concierge coordinate more than one part of a trip?

Some providers offer packages that bring several arrangements together. Energy Johnson focuses on private jet and yacht bookings with curated packages. Accommodation, transfers, dining, wellness and other requests can be discussed separately, subject to availability.
