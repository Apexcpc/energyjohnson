A PERSONALISED PROCESS

One point of contact,
the right experts.

We help map the conversations that matter, make relevant introductions and keep your priorities in view. Qualified providers confirm their own scope, eligibility requirements, fees and terms. Legal, tax, company formation and immigration advice remains with appropriately qualified independent specialists.

Clients who purchase a property through Energy Johnson receive personal relocation guidance at no additional Energy Johnson fee. This can include a tailored community brochure, area highlights and introductions for school options, private staffing, company set-up and other settling-in needs. Clients pay third-party providers directly. If you purchase elsewhere, we can discuss a tailored package and agree any Energy Johnson fee in advance.

COMMON QUESTIONS

A few useful
answers.

Every brief is individual. Use these points to frame your next conversation.

Can you help if I am moving alone or as a couple?

Yes. Support can be tailored to individuals, couples, families and business owners. School introductions are only relevant where requested.

Do you handle visa applications?

We can coordinate an introduction to an independent immigration specialist and help organise the wider move. The specialist advises on eligibility and applications; the relevant authority makes decisions.

Is relocation guidance included?

Clients who buy through Energy Johnson receive personal relocation guidance at no additional Energy Johnson fee. Third-party services may have their own charges, paid directly to providers. Other clients can discuss a bespoke package with any fee agreed in advance.
