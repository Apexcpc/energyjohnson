CLEAR ANSWERS, PERSONAL SUPPORT

A move made
with confidence.

How we can support your property search and life in Dubai.

Can you help me buy or rent a property in Dubai?

Yes. We support clients looking to buy or rent luxury homes in Dubai, including off-plan opportunities and properties already on the market. We shape the search around your preferred areas, lifestyle and priorities.

Can you help with off-plan and ready-to-move-in homes?

Yes. We can help you explore both off-plan properties and completed homes, coordinate relevant introductions and arrange viewings through our network.

Will you support us after we arrive in Dubai?

Yes. We can guide you through settling in, help you connect with relevant local specialists and remain your point of contact as your needs take shape.

Can you help with relocation and family needs?

We can coordinate introductions and guidance around your move, home search, school options and household staffing through specialist partners.

Can you arrange private jet and yacht bookings?

Yes. Private jet and yacht enquiries are handled personally. Share your dates, destination and preferences and we will discuss suitable options and availability.

How do I learn about the private retreats?

Retreats are a limited, invitation-led offering for a select circle of Energy Johnson clients. Enquire privately to learn about future destinations and availability.
