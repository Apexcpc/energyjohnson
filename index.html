CLEAR ANSWERS, PERSONAL SUPPORT

Frequently asked
questions.

Learn how we can support your next chapter.

What does Energy Johnson offer?

Energy Johnson coordinates wellness retreats, relocation support, private jet and yacht bookings, curated travel packages, and personal and business consultancy. Additional concierge arrangements can be discussed privately.

Where can you help?

We work across Dubai and a considered network of destinations, including Marbella, Bali, Finland, St. Moritz and Oman. Requests for other locations can be explored through our specialist network.

What relocation support is available for families?

We can help source a property that fits your preferred style, budget and location, explore school options for your children, and find suitable household staff through specialist partners. Legal, tax and company matters are referred to qualified independent professionals.

What should I include in an enquiry?

Tell us which service you are considering, your preferred destination and any timing or priorities you already have. We will use that information to shape an initial conversation.
