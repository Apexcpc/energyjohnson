COMPANY SET-UP & VISA COORDINATION

A clear process,
personal to you.

People come to Dubai to start a business, establish a regional base, relocate an existing company or understand which residence pathway may suit their situation. The right licence, legal structure, jurisdiction and visa route depend on your activity, ownership, plans and personal eligibility.

Energy Johnson accompanies the process: we listen to your priorities, help frame the questions and introduce you to independent company formation, legal, tax or immigration specialists as appropriate. Those specialists explain options, confirm requirements and handle regulated advice or applications within their own professional scope.

HOW WE ACCOMPANY YOU

Personal coordination,
specialist expertise.

Our role is to make the process feel more personal: understand your brief, coordinate introductions, help keep track of conversations and connect the company journey with the wider move when requested. We can help you prepare questions and clarify who is advising on each subject.

Company licensing, legal structuring, tax advice, immigration eligibility and visa applications are handled by qualified independent specialists and the relevant authorities. Their scope, availability, costs and terms are confirmed directly with you. We do not issue licences, provide legal or immigration advice, or guarantee an approval or timeline.

OFFICIAL STARTING POINTS

Check current
government guidance.

For company formation, begin with the official Invest in Dubai business set-up portal, which outlines mainland and free-zone options. For residence visas connected with doing business, review the UAE Government guidance. Requirements can change; confirm current details with the relevant authority and a qualified adviser before acting.

COMMON QUESTIONS

A few useful
answers.

Every brief is individual. These answers explain how we coordinate the first steps.

Can Energy Johnson open a company for me?

We accompany your process and introduce you to independent company formation specialists. The provider and relevant authority handle applications, approvals and licensing.

Can you help me understand visa options?

We can connect you with an independent immigration specialist and coordinate that conversation as part of your plans. The specialist confirms which pathways may apply and advises on eligibility and applications. Decisions are made by the relevant authorities.

How much do set-up or visa services cost?

Costs depend on your activity, jurisdiction, licence, visa route and provider. The independent specialist confirms scope, fees and government charges directly before you proceed.

Can you help with a home search too?

Yes. We can discuss property search and other relocation needs. Clients who buy a property through Energy Johnson receive personal relocation guidance at no additional Energy Johnson fee; third-party charges remain payable directly to each provider.
