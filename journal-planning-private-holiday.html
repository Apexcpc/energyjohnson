A private holiday
begins with the feeling.

The most memorable journeys start with a clear sense of what the time away should feel like. From that brief, the destination, stay and travel arrangements can take shape.

ENERGY JOHNSON JOURNAL

Good planning
starts with clarity.

Before choosing a hotel or route, consider who is travelling, the pace everyone enjoys and what you would like to remember. A family may value an easy rhythm and space to be together. A couple may want privacy and a few carefully chosen experiences. A group of friends may prefer a balance of time on the water, relaxed meals and a beautiful place to stay.

Energy Johnson helps arrange holidays around those preferences, with a particular focus on private jet and yacht bookings and curated travel packages. Forte Village, Sani Resort and Palazzo Fiuggi are examples of hotels we can arrange, not a limit on destinations or properties we can explore.

A BRIEF THAT FEELS PERSONAL

Share your people,
pace and priorities.

Useful details include preferred dates or season, destination ideas, party size, privacy needs, accessibility or dietary considerations, and the kind of moments you want to build around. If you do not have a destination in mind, share the setting or atmosphere you are drawn to. We can explore suitable options with you, subject to availability.

FROM DEPARTURE TO THE STAY

Bring the journey
together thoughtfully.

We focus on private jet and yacht bookings and can coordinate packages that connect the main parts of a journey. Accommodation, transfers, dining, wellness and activities can be discussed as part of the brief. Every provider confirms availability, final pricing, deposits, cancellation terms and the details of its service before a booking is final.

CLEAR FEES AND DIRECT PAYMENTS

Know how each
arrangement works.

For most holiday, hotel, jet and yacht bookings, Energy Johnson does not charge a separate booking fee; provider commissions may apply. Clients pay their chosen providers directly. A particular event or bespoke service may require a separate Energy Johnson organisation fee. If so, its scope and amount are explained and agreed in advance.
