BUSINESS · DUBAI

Starting a company
in Dubai.

A thoughtful company set-up begins with understanding what the business needs to do, where it will operate and which specialist can advise on the right route.

ENERGY JOHNSON JOURNAL

Good planning
starts with clarity.

Dubai attracts founders, investors and established businesses considering a new base. Before comparing licences or jurisdictions, it helps to describe the real operating plan: what the company will do, who it will serve, where its customers are and what presence it needs in the UAE.

Energy Johnson can accompany the wider process by helping you frame the brief, coordinate introductions and connect company planning with a property search or relocation where that is part of your plan. An independent company formation adviser confirms the available structures, requirements and costs for your case.

01 · DEFINE THE BUSINESS

Start with the activity,
not the paperwork.

Prepare a plain-language description of your planned activities, likely customers, ownership and expected operating footprint. If the business is moving from another country, explain what already exists and what needs to be established in Dubai. These points help a qualified adviser assess which licensing and jurisdiction questions to consider.

The official Invest in Dubai business set-up portal provides information on mainland and free-zone options. Use it as a starting point, then confirm the implications for your particular activity with an independent professional.

02 · DISCUSS THE WHOLE PICTURE

Company plans and
personal relocation.

For some clients, a new company is one part of a larger move. It may sit alongside a home search, residence visa questions, household staffing or family arrangements. Raising these topics early can help the relevant specialists clarify dependencies, sequencing and documents.

Visa eligibility and company formation rules depend on your circumstances and can change. Energy Johnson can coordinate introductions to independent specialists; those professionals advise on the options and any applications. Government authorities determine approvals. We do not issue licences or provide legal, tax or immigration advice.

03 · ASK ABOUT SCOPE AND COST

Know who is advising
on each decision.

Before proceeding, ask the provider what is included, which government charges may apply, what documents they need, how renewals are handled and which services sit outside their proposal. Ask who will be your contact and how they will keep you informed.

Energy Johnson can help coordinate the conversations around your broader plans. Specialist scope, fees, timelines and terms are confirmed directly between you and each provider.
