ABU DHABI

Know the place
before you choose.

Abu Dhabi is a separate emirate and the capital of the UAE, not a Dubai neighbourhood. Its residential choices span a wide geography and lifestyle range. Saadiyat Island is associated with cultural institutions, beaches and resorts; Yas Island combines homes with major leisure destinations; Al Reem Island offers a more urban, apartment-led setting; and Al Raha includes waterfront residential communities.

The right district is closely tied to where you work, how you spend your time, the schools or services you need, and how often you travel between emirates. A map distance alone may not reflect your real commute.

Is Abu Dhabi part of Dubai?

No. Abu Dhabi is a separate emirate and the capital of the UAE. Its neighbourhoods, commute patterns and property rules differ from Dubai.

Which Abu Dhabi area might suit my lifestyle?

It depends on where you need to be and what you value. Saadiyat has a cultural and coastal profile, Yas is known for leisure destinations, Reem has an urban residential character, and Al Raha offers waterfront communities. Visit specific homes and confirm current facilities.
