RELOCATION · DUBAI

A move to Dubai
that feels like yours.

Moving country is a series of personal decisions. A little structure can make the experience feel more welcoming, whether you are arriving alone, as a couple, with family or for business.

PUBLISHED BY ENERGY JOHNSON · 25 NOVEMBER 2026

ENERGY JOHNSON JOURNAL

Good planning
starts with clarity.

There is no single relocation checklist that fits everyone. Some people need a home before they arrive; others are establishing a company, exploring residence options or coordinating the move of an existing household. Families may also want to understand school choices, while individuals and couples may focus on location, privacy, commuting or the rhythm of daily life.

A useful first step is to separate decisions that need specialist advice from arrangements that can be coordinated around your preferences. Energy Johnson can be a personal point of contact and help connect the right conversations through a carefully selected network.

BEFORE YOU ARRIVE

Clarify the things
that shape daily life.

HOME

Where would life work best?

List the locations, property style, space and timing that matter to you. Explore whether buying, renting, off-plan or ready-to-move-in options suit your plans.

WORK

What needs to be established?

If a company set-up, employment or visa pathway is involved, note your questions and confirm them with qualified independent advisers.

PERSONAL

What would help you feel settled?

School options, household staffing, community information and introductions can be considered where relevant to your move.

COORDINATE AROUND YOUR NEEDS

A point of contact
through the transition.

Once you know your priorities, you can coordinate each workstream with the appropriate provider. Energy Johnson helps clients navigate the conversations, make relevant introductions and stay oriented as plans take shape. Legal, tax, company formation and immigration advice is supplied by qualified independent professionals, who confirm their own fees and terms.

Clients who purchase a property through Energy Johnson receive personal relocation guidance at no additional Energy Johnson fee. This may include a tailored community brochure, an explanation of area highlights and introductions to independent specialists. Clients pay any third-party providers directly. If you purchase elsewhere, a bespoke relocation package can be discussed and any Energy Johnson fee agreed in advance.

WHEN A FAMILY IS MOVING

School choices,
only when they matter.

If children are part of your move, you may want to compare schools by curriculum, location, admissions process and the needs of each child. We can introduce you to appropriate education specialists or help you explore the questions to ask. A school or specialist confirms its own criteria, availability, fees and admissions decisions. For current Dubai school information, consult the KHDA Parent Guides. For visa questions, use the UAE Government guidance and speak with a qualified adviser.

If you are moving without children, your relocation support can instead focus on the home, business, household or personal arrangements relevant to you.
