DUBAI HILLS ESTATE

Know the place
before you choose.

Dubai Hills Estate is a master-planned Emaar community with apartments, villas and townhouses set around open spaces, parkland and a championship golf course. Dubai Hills Mall and community amenities add to its self-contained feel, while the wider road network connects residents to other parts of the city.

Rather than one uniform neighbourhood, it is a collection of residential pockets. The distance from a home to a park, school, retail or a main route can shape daily life considerably.

THE CHARACTER

What gives it

Dubai Hills Estate has its own mix of setting, destinations and residential rhythms. The experience depends on the specific home and its immediate surroundings, so view the exact address and understand how you would use it day to day.

A FEW USEFUL QUESTIONS

Dubai Hills Estate
explained.

Every address and every plan deserves a closer look.

What kind of homes are in Dubai Hills Estate?

The community includes apartments, villas and townhouses. The mix, facilities and feel vary between sub-communities.

Is Dubai Hills Estate family-friendly?

Its parks, open spaces, schools and community amenities may appeal to families. Families should check the particular school choices, admission requirements and daily routes that matter to them.

What is the difference between Dubai Hills and Downtown?

Dubai Hills Estate is a large master-planned residential community with open spaces and varied home types. Downtown Dubai is a more landmark-led urban district with a more apartment-centred city environment.
