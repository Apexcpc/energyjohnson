A PRACTICAL ENERGY JOHNSON GUIDE

Off-plan or ready
for your next move?

There is no single right answer. The best route depends on when you want to move, what you value in a home and the details of the property or project.

PERSONAL GUIDANCE

Clear steps,
considered around you.

An off-plan home is purchased before construction is complete. A ready property is already built and can usually be viewed in its current condition, subject to access and availability. Each route raises different questions around timing, completion, contract terms, finishes and how soon you want to use the home.

Energy Johnson helps clients explore luxury homes to buy or rent, including off-plan opportunities and properties already on the market. We begin with your preferred location, lifestyle, property style and timing, then coordinate relevant introductions and viewings through our network.

COMPARE YOUR PRIORITIES

Choose around how
you plan to live.

OFF-PLAN

For a future move

Consider completion schedule, contract and payment terms, developer and project information, specifications and what the agreement says if plans change.

READY TO MOVE

For an immediate home

You may be able to view the actual property and assess its current condition. Confirm title, service costs, inclusions, handover status and repairs with appropriate professionals.

YOUR LIFESTYLE

For the right fit

Consider whether it is a primary residence, seasonal home or another purpose. Location, privacy, access and household needs may matter as much as timing.

QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU COMMIT

Look beyond
the images.

Ask who the seller or developer is, what is included, which costs apply, what the contract says about timing and completion, and who can independently review the terms. For off-plan projects, the Dubai Land Department provides a Project Status Enquiry and buyer information in its official FAQs.

These resources are a starting point, not a substitute for independent legal, financial, technical or property advice. Review transaction documents with appropriately qualified professionals before making a commitment.

COMMON QUESTIONS

A few useful
answers.

Every brief is individual. Use these points to frame your next conversation.

Is off-plan property always cheaper?

Pricing depends on the particular home, project, location, contract and market conditions. Compare the full cost and terms with an independent adviser rather than assuming one route is less expensive.

Can I move into an off-plan home immediately?

Usually the property must be completed and handed over before occupation. Confirm project status and contractual terms with the developer and your independent adviser.

How can I check a Dubai off-plan project?

Use the Dubai Land Department Project Status Enquiry to review project details and completion information. Have project documents and sale terms independently checked before signing or paying.

Can Energy Johnson help with both off-plan and ready homes?

Yes. We can discuss both options around your preferred location, style, needs and timing, and coordinate relevant introductions and viewings.
